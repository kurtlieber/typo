# Typo brand

Typo's mark is the logo of Kurt's earlier editor named typo ([kurtlieber/typo-archived](https://github.com/kurtlieber/typo-archived)), as lieber.org shows it: a lowercase t beside an amber text caret, on a dark tile. It replaced Quire's three-sheet mark in 2.6.2.

- **Mark and app icon:** `icon.svg`, 64 units square. A tile with 14-unit corners, shaded `#1F1F1F` to `#101010`, with a faint light edge. The t is two round-capped strokes, 5.5 units wide, in `#E6E6E6`. The caret is a 3.5 × 30 bar in amber `#E68E0D`, with a soft glow and a warm light behind it. The tile is the same on light and dark backgrounds.
- **Small sizes:** below 24 px use `icon-16.svg`. It drops the shading, the edge and the glow, and draws the strokes and the caret a little heavier.
- **Wordmark:** "typo" in lowercase Instrument Sans Semibold, tracking −4.5% (−0.045 em), line height 1.
- **Lockup:** mark and wordmark in a row. The gap is a quarter of the mark's width. At 52 px type the mark is 48 px with a 14 px gap.
- **App files:** `desktop/packaging/typo.svg` and `typo-16.svg` are copies of these two. `typo-512.png`, `typo.ico` and the macOS `typo.iconset` are rendered from them. The macOS icons put the tile on Apple's icon grid, 824 of 1,024 pixels with a clear margin.
